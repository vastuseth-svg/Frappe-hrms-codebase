REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP)

Enterprise HRMS SaaS Platform Development

Document Purpose

This document outlines the functional, technical, operational, and business requirements for the development and implementation of a comprehensive Enterprise Human Resource Management System (HRMS) SaaS Platform.

The purpose of this RFP is to establish a clear and mutually agreed scope of work to ensure successful delivery of the proposed HRMS solution.

1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Shree HR Service intends to implement a cloud-based, multi-tenant HRMS SaaS platform capable of serving multiple organizations through a centralized infrastructure while maintaining complete tenant-level data isolation.

The platform shall provide end-to-end employee lifecycle management including employee onboarding, attendance tracking, shift management, leave management, payroll processing, statutory compliance, performance management, asset management, workflow automation, reporting, mobile application access, Android kiosk attendance application for multi-employee capture, sales personnel face-mapping attendance, SaaS subscription management, and administrative controls.

The proposed solution must be scalable, secure, configurable, and suitable for organizations of varying sizes and industries.

Mandatory Cross-Day Attendance Logic

The system must support attendance spanning multiple calendar days.

Example:

Shift Timing: 08:00 AM to 08:00 PM

Employee Punch Out: 03:00 AM (next day)

Expected Behaviour:





Attendance shall continue against the original shift.



The system shall not create a new Punch-In automatically.



Additional hours worked shall be treated as Overtime.



Overtime must be calculated automatically.



A fresh attendance cycle shall start only when the employee reports for the next scheduled shift.



Night shifts and overnight shifts must be processed correctly without attendance duplication.

This requirement is mandatory.
